At 11 PM a wedding ends in Jaipur. The caterer has food for 100 people left over. Ten minutes away, a shelter has people who slept hungry that night. The food went in the dustbin — because nobody could connect them fast enough.

The process

From kitchen to shelter in under an hour

Three steps. No app to download. Works on any phone or computer.

1

Post your surplus

Caterer, hotel, hostel — fill a simple form. Tell us what food you have, how many it feeds, and where you are. Takes 60 seconds.

2

NGOs get alerted instantly

Every registered shelter within 20 km receives an email notification with your location, food details, and how much time is left.

3

Food reaches people

A shelter accepts and comes to collect. Cooked food listings expire after 12 hours for safety. Packed food stays live for 30 days.
